About Bombay Sofa & Furnishing

Bringing Comfort & Style Into Every Home.

Based in Silvassa, Dadra and Nagar Haveli, we are dedicated to helping families and homeowners create living spaces that balance sophisticated aesthetics with everyday comfort.

Our Story & Philosophy

A Destination for Furniture & Interior Furnishing in Silvassa

Bombay Sofa & Furnishing is established with a clear purpose: to provide homeowners in Silvassa and surrounding areas with high-quality seating, bedroom furniture, and window dressing without the compromises often found in mass-produced, off-the-shelf items.

We believe a sofa is more than just a piece of furniture—it is the heart of family conversations, relaxation after a long day, and the centerpiece of your home. That is why we provide complete customisation across frame dimensions, foam density, and upholstery textiles.

What We Do

Our Core Specialisations

Whether designing from scratch or rejuvenating existing furniture, our services cover every aspect of home comfort.

New Sofa Designs & Customisation

Custom-built L-shaped sectionals, 3+2+1 seating sets, curved designer couches, and armchairs tailored to your precise room geometry.

Sofa Reupholstery & Fabric Replacement

Give beloved sofas a complete visual and tactile makeover with our vast collection of velvets, textured linens, and stain-resistant fabrics.

Structural Sofa Repair

Fix sagging seats, broken wooden internal joints, worn webbings, and aging foam cores to restore structural strength.

Custom Beds & Headboards

Solid platform bed frames, hydraulic storage units, and extended upholstered headboards crafted to complement bedroom themes.

Curtains & Window Blinds

Complete window dressing with on-site measurement, American pleats, sheer overlays, and precision roller or zebra blinds.

Mattresses & Wallpapers

Custom-cut orthopedic mattresses for uninterrupted sleep alongside designer textured wallpapers for stunning feature walls.
