Our Services

Comprehensive Customisation & Repair Services

From building bespoke living room seating from your sketches to repairing spring tension and foam sagging.

1. Custom Sofa Designs

Have a particular space or vision? We construct brand-new sofas from scratch. Choose your exact frame dimensions, seat depth, armrest style, and comfort firmness.

2. Sofa Repair

Restore weak or damaged structures. We repair cracked wooden frames, reinforce loosened joints, replace broken serpentine springs, and fix sagging seat bases.

3. Sofa Reupholstery

Complete transformation of the inner and outer materials. We strip worn fabric, inspect internal strapping, re-pad with fresh high-density foam, and re-tailor.

4. Sofa Fabric Replacement

When internal cushioning is sound but the exterior is stained, torn, or outmoded. Select from 500+ modern velvets, textured jacquards, and pet-friendly textiles.

5. Furniture Customisation

Beyond sofas, we customize upholstered platform beds, matching bedroom headboards, dining chair cushioning, ottomans, and bespoke living room coffee tables.
